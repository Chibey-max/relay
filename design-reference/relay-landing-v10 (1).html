Questions you'd actually ask

Straight answers, plainly put.

What exactly happens when I hit send?+
Relay reads your sentence, works out who and how much, finds where your funds already sit, and moves them — no chain picker, no gas step for you to fill in.
Where does my money actually live?+
In a wallet only you control, created the moment you log in with your email. Relay never holds your funds.
Do I need a seed phrase?+
No. You log in with email, and your wallet is created for you behind the scenes.
What does this cost?+
Nothing extra right now — every network fee is sponsored while this is live.
Is this really on-chain, or a demo?+
Both exist in the app, and each is clearly labeled. Two real sends are already public on Arbitrum — you can open them directly from this page.
What if my funds aren't already on Arbitrum?+
Today, Relay settles from funds already unified onto Arbitrum. Sourcing directly from other chains in the same send is the next thing being built — nothing here claims that's live yet.
